# Phase 1 data coverage audit

This is the historical Phase 1 audit. For current permission decisions and verified
Phase 3 geography, see [data access and readiness](DATA_ACCESS.md).

Audited 4 October 2026. The canonical area-by-metric evidence is [coverage_audit.csv](../data/manifests/coverage_audit.csv): 16 municipalities (12 contrasting cities and Getafe, Badalona, Mislata, Dos Hermanas), each assessed for sale €/m², whole-home rent €/m²/month, and room €/month. The CSV's `numeric_verified` means a value and its period/meaning were checked in a linked primary publication; it does **not** grant redistribution rights. `publication_verified` means the city is discussed without an absolute value. `not_verified` means no suitable value was established in this bounded audit. Blank means unknown, never zero. Codes marked `provisional` need reconciliation with the current INE municipal-code file before a data release.

## Verified scope and pilot

Idealista's public [sale](https://www.idealista.com/sala-de-prensa/informes-precio-vivienda/venta/report/) and [rental](https://www.idealista.com/sala-de-prensa/informes-precio-vivienda/alquiler/report/) reports give September 2026 municipality/city asking-price indicators for all 12 selected cities. Province report pages give the four surrounding municipalities (linked per CSV row). These are pooled residential market indicators, not ordinary-existing-flat, 80 m², two-bedroom, or transaction-specific prices. Whole-home rent is €/m²/month, not a ready-made monthly bill for an arbitrary flat. The [July 2026 methodology](https://st3.idealista.com/cms/archivos/static/price-indicator/es-metodologia-informes-de-precios-vivienda-2026.pdf) describes a weighted aggregation of zonal medians, based on visible asking listings, and possible estimates for low-support zones. It excludes temporary rentals from its conventional rental indicator. These series must not be labelled transaction prices or tax-declared rents.

The [Q2 2026 room release](https://www.idealista.com/sala-de-prensa/notas-de-prensa/2026/08/24/266632-la-oferta-de-habitaciones-se-dispara-un-12-y-el-precio-se-queda-casi-plano) provides actual mean city room rents for Barcelona (€600/month), Madrid and Palma (€550), Bilbao and Málaga (€450), and Jaén (€250). It discusses Valencia, Sevilla and Teruel without an absolute figure; the remaining sampled areas have no room value established by this report. In particular, no surrounding-municipality room figure is verified. This is a different period and product from September whole-home rent. Room bills and size/quality segmentation are unresolved.

**Recommended Phase 3 pilot:** municipality-level Madrid, Barcelona, and Jaén for a deliberately narrow room/flat/sale comparison, subject to redistribution rights and source-method checks. They span markedly different price levels and have all three headline metrics in public reports. Add surrounding municipalities only for purchase and whole-home-rent comparisons until room values are sourced. The map must label unsupported room cases as missing and never derive them from a universal flat-rent multiplier. City/municipality report rows should be joined only after their official identifiers and boundary versions are reconciled.

## Other routes and access

- The [CNIG boundary catalogue](https://centrodedescargas.cnig.es/CentroDescargas/limites-municipales-provinciales-autonomicos) lists versioned municipal, provincial and autonomous-community polygons and says its geographic-data licence is compatible with CC-BY 4.0. It offers a roughly 157 MB Shapefile; Phase 1 inspected the listing and licence statement, not the file or its exact code column. The [INE municipality-code operation](https://www.ine.es/daco/daco42/codmun/codmun_anual.htm) is the official identifier reference; the full 2026 code catalogue was not obtained here. Several codes in the CSV are therefore marked provisional. The historical INE references used for some codes do not substitute for a current boundary join check.
- The public [SERPAVI reference site](https://serpavi.mivau.gob.es/) was reachable as a property/address lookup. The brief's MIVAU statistics page returned an access error through this audit route, so a bulk municipal table, its period and reuse terms were **not verified**. Tax-declared or contract-based history would answer a different question from current entrant asking rent even if obtained.
- The [MIVAU valuation bulletin](https://apps.fomento.gob.es/boletinonline2/?nivel=2&orden=35000000) lists values for municipalities over 25,000 people, but the linked XLS was not retrieved through the audit route. Valuations cannot silently replace asking sale prices.
- [Idealista/data](https://www.idealista.com/data/asesoramiento-inmobiliario-tecnologico/api-comparables-y-metricas/) describes API, widgets and exports and invites provider contact. No unrestricted API, contract, room coverage or redistribution grant was verified. The public methodology says its report content is protected and requires source attribution. The audit commits only a small attributed sample and references; permission to redistribute a full derived release remains unknown.

## Readiness

Phase 1 has enough primary evidence to define the contracts and a restricted pilot; Phase 3 is **not ready to publish a reusable market release**. Before building one, confirm the right to redistribute the chosen Idealista samples, reconcile current INE/CNIG identifiers and boundaries, and decide whether the limited six-city public room coverage is acceptable. If broader room coverage is required, ask the provider for a licensed source or choose another verified route. No collection job, production dataset or geometries were created in this phase. The JSON market fixture under `tests/fixtures/synthetic` is wholly synthetic and must never be read as an observed market release.
